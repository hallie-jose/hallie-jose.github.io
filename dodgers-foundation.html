LA Dodgers Foundation

League Administration Associate · 2016 to 2018

Hallie's career in sports business started here, at the Los Angeles Dodgers Foundation, where she supported the Dodgers RBI program (Reviving Baseball in Inner Cities) and the Foundation's broader community and fundraising work.

She produced creative assets, including flyers, graphics, and campaign copy, supporting Dodgers RBI programming and LADF fundraising initiatives such as the Blue Diamond Gala, and partnered in the planning and execution of Dodgers RBI youth sports programming and Dodger Day community festivals across LA-area parks.

On the data side, she analyzed donor trends and participant engagement metrics, delivering data-backed insights that informed sponsor and fundraising strategy, and partnered directly with the Foundation's CMO on fundraising campaign strategy, strengthening donor relationships and driving revenue-generating events.

It's where she first learned that community work and business strategy aren't separate skill sets, a lesson that's shaped everything she's built since.

  • Program
    Dodgers RBI (Reviving Baseball in Inner Cities)
  • Events
    Dodger Day community festivals, the Blue Diamond Gala
  • Focus

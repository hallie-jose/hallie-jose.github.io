We Go Healthy Co.

Founder, Marketing Director & Head Performance Coach · 2020 to Present

We Go Healthy is the strength and performance coaching business Hallie founded in 2020, an online and in-person operation delivering personalized training programs, nutrition-informed guidance, and race-specific programming for everyday athletes and endurance runners.

She built the client-facing platform herself, on Trainerize, housing the complete library of performance-based training programming for every client, informed by knowledge accumulated through collaboration with clinics including Dang Physical Therapy and Mamba Sports Academy.

On the program design side, she created a multi-phase Marathon Prep Series for the LA, Long Beach, Honolulu, and CIM marathons, along with rehab-integrated strength and speed programming for marathoners, triathletes, dancers, combat athletes, and climbers.

In 2023 she secured in-person facility space at Movement Society in DTLA to host specialized coaching, kettlebell group training, and hybrid run-strength sessions, the physical counterpart to the online platform.

She also built and runs the organic brand and content strategy for the business herself, under the handle @coach.halliejose on Instagram and TikTok, alongside full-funnel Meta ad campaigns and an affiliate/influencer program.

Six-plus years in, the business has grown into a fully operational, multi-channel operation, entirely self-funded, with zero outside investment.

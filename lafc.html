Los Angeles Football Club

Business & Data Strategy Assistant · 2019 to 2020

At LAFC, Hallie worked on the business and data side of the club's partnerships program, using Tableau, Salesforce, Service Console, and Kore CRM to deliver in-depth partner performance analytics that informed strategic decision-making across the partnerships team.

She collaborated cross-functionally to conceptualize and execute strategic partner activations, including social media campaigns and in-stadium fan experiences, and directed the strategic mapping of stadium signage placement to optimize visibility for current and prospective partners.

On the data side, she designed and deployed fan surveys assessing partner giveaways, marketing effectiveness, and stadium assets, distributed leaguewide after every match, then synthesized the responses into custom Tableau dashboards that shaped partnership strategy.

She also engineered a dynamic Excel-based impression value model, quantifying partner asset performance by attendance and visualizing the results through Tableau for executive-level reporting, the analytical throughline connecting her sports-business work at LAFC to the partnership work she does today.

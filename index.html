HALLIE JOSE

Strategic PartnershipsCommunityMarket Activations

Hallie Jose has spent the last decade building community at the intersection of sports, fitness, and brand partnerships. It started at the Los Angeles Dodgers Foundation, where she supported the Dodgers RBI program and donor-facing campaigns like the Blue Diamond Gala, helping strengthen the donor relationships that kept RBI programming funded for LA youth, an early lesson in how much of business really comes down to people.

From there she moved to LAFC, where she helped shape strategic partner activations and built the reporting models partners used to evaluate their own performance, models the team relied on directly in renewal conversations. It's where she first learned to pair the technical side, data, dashboards, reporting, with the relationship-building that actually gets a partner to renew.

In 2020 she founded We Go Healthy, an online and in-person coaching business built entirely on her own client relationships and programming, informed by collaboration with clinics like Dang Physical Therapy and Mamba Sports Academy. Her clients have ranged from busy professionals and high performers, engineers, business owners, senior managers at JPL, doctors, and the work has evolved right alongside them, from everyday lifestyle goals like weight loss and strength into performance-based running, where she now works primarily as a strength coach for runners chasing finish lines in LA, New York, Valencia, and Chicago. In six years, she's coached 1,000-plus people, with roughly half sticking with her long-term, not because of a workout plan, but because she taught them to trust their own consistency again.

In 2024 she founded Run the Blocks, an Arts District run and strength community that grew to 100-plus weekly members and 250-plus Strava members in just four months. The programming stayed performance-heavy from day one, real training, real races, but the partnerships followed the community rather than the other way around: brands like Vuori, On, Adidas, and Zappos came calling because the events were ones people actually wanted to show up to, some pulling 200-plus people out for a single night, and more than that, a place where members have built real friendships, not just workout buddies.

Along the way she's built a rare combination of skills: the interpersonal instincts of a coach and community builder, and the technical, deal-structuring, data-driven side of a partnerships operator. As one manager put it: "It's rare to find someone who strikes such a perfect balance between soft and hard skills at this point in their career. Hallie's unique combination of interpersonal and technical skills consistently allows her to operate above her level and provide outsized business impact."

She's now pivoting into her next chapter: scaling the impact she's made at the local level to a broader, global stage. The throughline across every chapter has been the same, take a community or a client from where they are to a goal that once felt out of reach, whether that's a first 5K or a signed brand deal, and she's ready to bring that same combination of relationship-building and systems-thinking into a role where she can help shape the infrastructure that empowers millions of athletes, not just the hundreds she's reached directly so far.

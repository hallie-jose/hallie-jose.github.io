Hallie Jose has spent the last decade building community at the intersection of sports, fitness, and brand partnerships. It started at the Los Angeles Dodgers Foundation, where she supported the Dodgers RBI program and donor-facing campaigns like the Blue Diamond Gala, helping strengthen the donor relationships that kept RBI programming funded for LA youth.

From there she moved to LAFC, where she helped shape strategic partner activations and built the reporting models partners used to evaluate their own performance, models the team relied on directly in renewal conversations, her first real look at the deal-making side of sports business.

In 2020 she founded We Go Healthy, an online and in-person coaching business built entirely on her own client relationships and programming, informed by collaboration with clinics like Dang Physical Therapy and Mamba Sports Academy. She built the platform herself, on Trainerize, and has kept a roster of 30-plus concurrent clients moving toward real goals for six years running, with roughly half sticking with her long-term.

In 2024 she founded Run the Blocks, an Arts District run and strength community that grew to 100-plus weekly members and 250-plus Strava members in just four months. It became a real activation partner for brands like Vuori, On, Adidas, and Zappos, not through paid placements, but through events people actually wanted to show up to, some pulling 200-plus people out for a single night.

She's now pivoting into her next chapter: scaling the impact she's made at the local level to a broader, global stage. Years of direct coaching and hands-on community building taught her how to build things people actually want to be part of, and she's ready to bring that into a role where she can shape the systems that empower millions of athletes.

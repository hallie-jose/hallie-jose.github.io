HALLIE JOSE

Creative Strategy→Community Building→Strategic Partnerships

Hallie Jose has spent the last decade building community at the intersection of sports, fitness, and brand partnerships. It started at the Los Angeles Dodgers Foundation, where she supported the Dodgers RBI (Reviving Baseball in Inner Cities) program and donor-facing campaigns like the Blue Diamond Gala, helping strengthen the donor relationships that kept RBI programming funded for LA youth. This was an early lesson in how much of business really comes down to people.

From there she moved to Los Angeles Football Club, where she helped shape strategic activations for the team's corporate partners and built the reporting models those partners used to evaluate their own performance. The team relied on these models directly in renewal conversations. It's where she first learned to pair the technical side, data, dashboards, and reporting, with the relationship-building that successfully brings partners back to renew.

In 2020, Hallie founded We Go Healthy, an online and in-person coaching business built entirely on her own client relationships and programming, shaped by collaboration with clinics like Dang Physical Therapy and Mamba Sports Academy. Her clients have ranged from busy professionals to performing athletes, and the work has evolved right alongside them. She provided concrete, sustainable, and positive support for clients working toward everyday lifestyle goals like weight loss, as well as more performance-focused goals like training for marathons and half-marathons. Her focus eventually turned to performance-based running, where she now works primarily as a strength coach for distance runners. She's proud to have helped clients complete, and in some cases break their personal records in, races including the LA Marathon, the New York Marathon, the Valencia Marathon in Spain, and the Chicago Marathon. In six years, she's coached 1,000+ people, with roughly half sticking with her long-term, not because of a workout plan, but because she taught them to trust their own consistency again.

In 2024, Hallie founded Run the Blocks, an Arts District run and strength community in LA that grew to 100+ weekly members and 250+ Strava members in just four months. The programming stayed performance-heavy from day one, real training, real races, but the partnerships eventually followed the community rather than the other way around: brands like Vuori, On, Adidas, and Zappos reached out to collaborate, recognizing that Run the Blocks events garnered huge community support, at times pulling 200+ people out for a single night. Run the Blocks has accomplished more than that, too. It's created a welcoming space where members have not only gained workout and running partners, but also built real friendships.

Along the way, Hallie's built a rare combination of skills: the interpersonal instincts of a coach and community builder, and the technical, deal-structuring, data-driven side of a partnerships operator. As one manager put it: "It's rare to find someone who strikes such a perfect balance between soft and hard skills at this point in their career. Hallie's unique combination of interpersonal and technical skills consistently allows her to operate above her level and provide outsized business impact."

Hallie's now pivoting into her next chapter: scaling the impact she's made at the local level to a broader, global stage. The throughline across every chapter has been the same: take a community or a client from where they are to a goal that once felt out of reach, whether that's a signed brand deal or a first 5K. She's ready and excited to bring that same combination of relationship-building and systems-thinking into a role where she can help shape the infrastructure that empowers millions of athletes, not just the hundreds she's reached directly so far.
